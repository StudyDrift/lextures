# Lextures off-site authenticity policy

Owner: Chase Willden  
Effective: 2026-08-11  
Review cadence: quarterly

## Required disclosure

Every post, reply, review response, comment, or contribution made for Lextures must say in its own
body: **“I work at Lextures — …”** Personal profiles and signatures do not replace this disclosure.
Answer the question first. Mention Lextures only when directly relevant, and link only when the
destination is the best available answer. Read and record a community's rules before participating.

## Prohibited conduct

Never use undisclosed or alternate accounts, sockpuppets, fake testimonials, sentiment-filtered
review requests, compensated or incentivized reviews, paid do-follow links, link exchanges, or copy
written to appear independent. Do not suppress a negative review. Dispute one only through the
platform's documented process when it contains a factual error.

## Reviews and public data

Review invitations go to a defined cohort such as every customer past 60 days, without filtering by
health score, support history, survey response, or predicted sentiment. The request log records the
cohort definition and date. Customer names, quotes, case studies, and school details require written
consent in the unified consent ledger. Never disclose student or school data.

## Accountability and escalation

Register every company-use account in `accounts.md` before posting. Shared access requires MFA and a
password manager or SSO; credentials never enter this repository. If anyone asks for undisclosed
promotion, selective review solicitation, a purchased link, or publication of customer data, do not
proceed and escalate to the owner. Correct an accidental omission publicly and record it in the
account or mention register.

# International SEO locale priority gate

Status: evidence pending; no non-English locale is approved for publication.

The architecture allowlists `es`, `fr-CA`, and `en-GB` as planned candidates, but does not route them. Choosing a first locale requires three months of comparable evidence from Search Console country/query data, product locale usage, qualified sales pipeline, and confirmed product-language coverage. The decision owner must also confirm native education-domain review capacity and, for localized trust claims, jurisdictional compliance review.

Record the decision here with the analysis date, source exports, sample sizes, candidate scores, reviewer, product-support confirmation, translation budget, and compliance sign-off. Do not change a locale to `published` before that record exists. This avoids presenting guessed demand or unreviewed translations as evidence.

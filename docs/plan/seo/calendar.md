Each research slot reserves the next four weekly non-rapid-response slots for derivative articles.
Report #1 derivatives cover mastery, time-to-mastery, retention, and study limitations; report #2
derivatives cover adoption, abandonment, segment differences, and outcome associations. Exact titles
are locked only after reviewed findings exist so the calendar cannot preselect a favorable story.

Pillar pages only enter the route manifest after five published cluster articles exist. A “launch review” is therefore a gate, not a guaranteed publication date.

## Status rules

Allowed states are `briefed`, `drafted`, `in review`, `published`, `missed`, and `reserved`. A missed row must retain its date and explain why. Before publication, the editor records a second-person review, verifies three primary sources, confirms a score of at least 8.0, and checks the article-to-pillar and contextual links. Quarterly refresh selections and pruning decisions go in [performance.md](performance.md).

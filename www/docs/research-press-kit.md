# Research press-kit template

Each report's `public/research/<slug>/press-kit/` directory contains:

- `summary.md`: one paragraph, research contact, permanent report URL, version, and CC BY 4.0 notice;
- `headline-statistics.csv`: three to five exact claims and their dataset `figure_id` values;
- light and dark PNG/SVG chart images with adjacent alt-text files;
- the same chart data as accessible CSV tables;
- correction and response contact, with a two-business-day response target.

Wording is locked to the reviewed dataset. Do not round, extrapolate, imply causality, or substitute a
different population in outreach copy.

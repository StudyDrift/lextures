# Comparison page policy

Lextures comparison and alternatives pages use competitor names only to identify the products being evaluated. They do not use competitor logos, wordmarks, trade dress, review schema, or unsourced characterizations.

Capability claims must link to the vendor's own public documentation and record an access date. If a capability cannot be verified, write “Not documented publicly”; do not convert uncertainty into “No.” Pricing is quoted only from a vendor's own current pricing page. Quote-only pricing is described as not public and is never estimated.

Every page must say that Lextures is the publisher, include at least two genuine competitor strengths, display its last-verified date, and provide a visible changelog. Reverify pages quarterly. A page older than 120 days enters the SEO lifecycle report and remains visibly dated until reviewed.

Send corrections to corrections@lextures.com. Acknowledge a request within two business days and resolve or answer it within five business days. Record any material correction in the page changelog. Legal review is required before the first template launch and before naming a competitor security incident.

Page owners must keep Article, BreadcrumbList, and FAQPage structured data consistent with visible content. Integration pages also use HowTo and SoftwareApplication data. Never emit Review, AggregateRating, or competitor Product schema.

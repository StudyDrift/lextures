# Primary sources policy

Use peer-reviewed papers, government datasets, standards bodies, statutes, or the vendor documentation that defines the claim. Do not use an aggregator or another marketing blog when the original source is available.

Place `[^1]` immediately after a numeric, research, legal, or standards claim and define it at the end with an HTTPS URL, title, publisher, and access date. List the same canonical URLs in front matter `citations` so Article schema receives them. Competitor claims must link to that competitor's own public documentation and record the access date. Editors re-verify those claims during the scheduled content refresh.

Pages making accessibility, privacy, FERPA, GDPR, or WCAG claims also require `reviewedBy` and `reviewedAt`; the reviewer must be the responsible compliance owner.

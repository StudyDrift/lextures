# Editorial process

The calendar in `docs/plan/seo/calendar.md` is the source of truth. Marketing owns the queue; the named author owns factual accuracy; a second human owns the publication review. Keep ten non-reserved publication slots (about eight weeks) briefed and assigned.

1. Research the verbatim question in a keyword provider, Google SERP, and the six tracked assistants. Save dated evidence in the brief. Re-angle saturated questions.
2. Score the opportunity with the documented formula. The content lead selects work by score, seasonality, cluster coverage, and available expertise.
3. Draft to the answer-first contract. Link to the pillar, at least three contextual internal pages, and at least three primary sources.
4. A second person verifies sources, claims, consent, accessibility, score ≥8.0, metadata, and `reviewDue`. Compliance-adjacent claims get a specialist review.
5. Publish only on the calendar date. Mark a missed slot with its reason rather than shipping filler. Submit the URL through the existing indexing and distribution workflow.
6. Record impressions, clicks, rank, internal links, AI citations, assisted conversions, and time to rank monthly. AI-related content is reviewed in six months; other content defaults to twelve.
7. Quarterly, refresh the ten highest-traffic and ten declining high-potential pages. Improve or consolidate pages with no impressions, citation, or internal-link value after six months; log every 301 decision.

Run `npm run content:calendar`, `npm run content:gaps`, and `npm run content:refresh-due` from `www/`. The pillar route launch gate is five published cluster articles; never add placeholders to make a hub appear larger.

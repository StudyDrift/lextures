# Competitor research workflow

1. Define the exact capability-level question before researching it.
2. Find the vendor's own documentation or official pricing page over a third-party summary.
3. Record the URL, access date, supported version, region, plan, and any configuration dependency.
4. Write a falsifiable statement that stays within the source. Avoid qualitative labels such as “clunky,” “modern,” or “outdated.”
5. Have a second reviewer open every source and confirm that the on-page statement is supported.
6. Test links, schema, responsive tables, accessibility, and static HTML in the production build.
7. Update the verification date and changelog. Recheck within 120 days.

Third-party sources may support adoption or satisfaction context, but not capability claims. Do not infer missing support from silence. Do not name a customer without written consent. Escalate security-incident language, ambiguous trademark use, and disputed claims to legal before publication.

# Standards licensing and attribution

The initial browser covers a small NGSS subset. Lextures does not reproduce official performance-expectation text: it publishes an original summary and original mastery and assessment guidance with a direct official link. NGSS and related marks belong to their owners; inclusion does not imply endorsement.

Before adding a framework, record its source, licence terms, attribution, redistribution decision, reviewer, and date in `src/data/standards-sources.ts`. If permission is unclear, render only an original summary and official link. No standard is generated without original mastery, assessment, and misconception content.

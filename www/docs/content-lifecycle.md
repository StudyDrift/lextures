# Content lifecycle

Every new blog, help, comparison, or research page has an owner and review due date. Blog and help
articles are monitored in the Marketing Content workspace Health and Governance views; weekly owners
review overdue items and the overdue-review ratio. Repository-native comparison and research pages
retain their page-specific checks.

At review, choose one outcome: refresh and advance `updated`/`reviewDue`; consolidate into a stronger page and add a permanent redirect; or retire with HTTP 410 and sitemap removal. Never silently delete a published URL. Record consolidations, retirements, exceptions, and owners in the lifecycle log.

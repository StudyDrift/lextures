# External contributor guide

Contributors add first-hand professional experience, not promotional copy. Agree on the brief, scope, payment, byline, editing rights, and deadline before work begins. Use plain English, distinguish observation from research, and provide a primary source for every material factual or numeric claim.

Do not include student data, identifiable classroom examples, customer names, screenshots, or institutional quotes without written permission in the consent ledger. FERPA, GDPR, WCAG, security, or legal claims require the assigned specialist review. Authors may use drafting tools, but a human must verify every claim and remains accountable for the published work.

Submit content through the repository’s allowlisted Markdown workflow. Raw HTML, scripts, embeds, and unapproved components are rejected. The content lead performs structural editing; a second reviewer checks evidence and meaning. Publication is conditional on the answer-first score, source checks, accessibility, consent, and the calendar—not on filling a quota.

After publication, contributors may share the canonical URL and must disclose their relationship to Lextures. Material corrections are welcome at any time; otherwise AI-related pieces are reviewed after six months and other pieces after twelve.

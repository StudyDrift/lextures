# A week you can actually finish

Pick three priorities for the week. Write them where the learner will see them: a notebook page, a sticky note, or the course todo list. Everything else can wait.

## A study block

1. Set a timer for 25 minutes.
2. Work on one priority. Close extra tabs.
3. When the timer ends, stop. Write one sentence about what is still unfinished.

## A two-minute review

At the end of the day, ask: what did we finish, and what moves to tomorrow? That review is the whole point of the plan. A short list you revisit beats a long list you ignore.
